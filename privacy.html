Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

Webara Digital respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we may receive when you use this website, contact us, or enquire about our services.

Information you provide

If you use our enquiry form, we may receive your name, email address and enquiry details. Submitting the form opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message addressed to our configured business contact. The website itself does not store the form submission in a database.

Information collected automatically

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type and request data, in order to deliver and secure the service. We do not currently operate advertising cookies or behavioural tracking on this website.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss potential projects.
  • To provide proposals, quotes and relevant business information.
  • To maintain the security and operation of the website.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Third-party services

Links and actions on this website may take you to third-party services, including WhatsApp, GitHub Pages and Google Fonts. Those services process information under their own privacy policies. We do not control their practices.

Retention and security

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential project or meet legal and accounting requirements. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact oswald@cryptosi.org or message us on WhatsApp.

This page provides general information about our current website practices and is not a substitute for legal advice. We may update this policy when our services, tools or legal obligations change.
