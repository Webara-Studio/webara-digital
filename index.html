What is a website audit — and why does your business need one?

A website audit is a full health check of your website. We test it the way real visitors and Google actually experience it — on real phones, on real networks — and turn the findings into a plain-English report: what is working, what is costing you customers, and exactly what to fix first.

Here is why it matters. Most visitors decide in seconds. If your site is slow, hard to read on a phone, or invisible on Google, they leave and buy from someone else — usually without you ever knowing they came. An audit finds those silent problems and turns them into a prioritised fix list, so you spend money on the changes that actually grow the business.

  • You get facts, not opinions. Industry-standard scores for speed, usability, best practices and search — measured, not guessed.
  • You see what customers endure. How long your page really takes on a typical phone in Ghana, and what makes people give up.
  • You know exactly what to fix first. Every finding comes with a plain-English explanation and a fix-first action plan, ranked by impact.
  • You stop losing customers you can't see. Faster pages, clearer navigation and better search visibility bring in visitors who would have silently bounced.
  • It pays for itself. Fixing what an audit finds is almost always cheaper than rebuilding — and cheaper than the customers you're quietly losing every month.
